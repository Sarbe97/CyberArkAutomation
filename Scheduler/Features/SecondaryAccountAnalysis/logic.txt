1. Data Collection (Gathering the Raw Data)

Active Directory: Pulls all Primary users (U accounts) from the NA domain.
Active Directory: Pulls all Secondary accounts (A, S, etc.) from all configured domains.
Active Directory: Pulls all members of the kapamprodusers group.
CyberArk: Pulls the names of all existing Safes and all Onboarded Accounts.
2. Analysis (Checking the Rules) (Loops through every secondary account to assign it exactly one of these statuses):

MissingPrimary: It has no matching Primary user. (Action: Skip)
PrimaryDisabled: The Primary user is disabled in AD. (Action: Skip)
MissingGroupAccess: The Primary user is NOT in the kapamprodusers group. (Action: Skip)
Managed: The secondary account is already onboarded in CyberArk. (Action: Skip, nothing to do)
NeedsOnboarding: The personal safe already exists, but the account is not in it. (Action: Ready for Onboarding)
NeedsAll: The personal safe does not exist, and the account is not onboarded. (Action: Ready for Safe Creation + Onboarding)
3. Execution (Doing the Work) (Only applies to accounts flagged as NeedsOnboarding or NeedsAll)

Create Safe: Creates the personal safe in CyberArk and adds the required members.
Onboard Account: Adds the secondary account to the safe.
Send User Email: After all accounts are finished, the script groups the successful accounts by user and sends one "Welcome to your Vault" email to the Primary User's email address (listing all the secondary accounts that were just added for them).
4. Admin Reporting (The Final Recap)

Sends one Run Summary email to the Admins.
Attaches 4 CSVs:
OnboardingResults.csv (What safes were actually created & accounts onboarded)
MissingGroupAccess.csv (Users who need to be added to AD group)
SkippedAccounts.csv (Users skipped because primary is missing or disabled)
AnalysisReport.csv (The master list of all 15,000+ accounts and their statuses)
